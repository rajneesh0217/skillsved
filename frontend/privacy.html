1. Introduction

Welcome to Skillsved (https://skillsved.in), an educational brand operated by Learning Bliss Edtech, a registered business in India.

At Skillsved, we respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and manage information provided through our website and communication channels.

This policy describes our data-handling practices when you visit our website or interact with our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when submitting an enquiry, requesting course information or contacting Skillsved.

The information you provide will be used to respond to your enquiries, share relevant course and learning-related information, assist with enrolment, and provide educational support.

This information may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Contact number
  • City or location
  • Educational or professional background
  • Course or program of interest
  • Learning goals and expectations
  • Information voluntarily shared during telephone calls, emails or other communications

Our website and hosting providers may also process limited technical information, such as IP addresses and browser-related information, for website operation, security and abuse prevention.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for legitimate educational and business purposes, including:

  • Responding to course enquiries and information requests.
  • Providing details about programs, admissions, schedules and fees.
  • Contacting interested individuals through telephone calls, WhatsApp or email regarding relevant Skillsved courses, webinars and educational services.
  • Providing enrolment assistance and learner support.
  • Maintaining enquiry and communication records.
  • Improving our website, services and user experience.
  • Protecting our website against spam, misuse and unauthorized activity.

We contact individuals who voluntarily submit enquiries or communicate with Skillsved. You may ask us to stop promotional follow-ups at any time.

4. Consent and Communication Preferences

When you voluntarily submit an enquiry or contact Skillsved, we may use the information provided to respond and communicate with you about your expressed interests.

Where additional consent is required by applicable law, we will seek that consent.

You may request that we stop sending promotional communications by contacting info@skillsved.in.

Opting out of promotional messages will not prevent us from sending essential communications relating to an existing enrolment, transaction or service request.

5. Sharing of Personal Information

Skillsved does not sell personal information or share enquiry details with external marketing agencies, freelance counsellors or third-party lead-generation companies.

Access to personal information is limited to authorized personnel who need it for legitimate business activities.

We may use trusted technology providers for website hosting, database storage, email forwarding and other essential operations. These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a lawful authority.

6. Data Storage and Security

Skillsved takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss and disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, protected administrative systems and security features provided by our technology partners.

Although we work to safeguard information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for managing enquiries, maintaining communication records and providing relevant educational assistance.

We do not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion period to enquiry records.

Individuals may request deletion of their personal information at any time. Following receipt and appropriate verification of a request, we will delete or anonymize relevant information within a reasonable period, unless continued retention is necessary for legal compliance, dispute resolution or other lawful obligations.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Requests

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to:

  • Request information about the personal data we hold concerning you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent for applicable processing activities.
  • Stop receiving promotional communications.
  • Raise a privacy-related concern or complaint.

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website and its technology providers may use essential technical mechanisms needed for website functionality, security and performance.

If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising cookies or tracking technologies, we will review and update our privacy disclosures and consent mechanisms as required.

10. Third-Party Platforms

Skillsved may communicate with individuals through services such as Gmail, WhatsApp or other communication platforms.

These services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. We encourage users to review the relevant policies when interacting through third-party platforms.

11. Children's Privacy

Skillsved's courses and enquiry services are intended primarily for adults and individuals eligible to participate in our educational programs.

Where personal information relating to a child is processed, we will follow applicable legal requirements, including any necessary parental or guardian consent.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy
